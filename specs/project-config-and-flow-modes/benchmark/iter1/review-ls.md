## Moment: prototype review — http://localhost:3101/prototypes/linkedin-sync (winner `VariantVetrina.tsx` + `ExpandCard.tsx`/`MorphView.tsx`; real target `/profile/company/settings`)
## Verdict: iterate — 2 small changes, both about how the card behaves during and after a sync. The fake sync takes 2.4 s and hides that the real one runs up to ~120 s with no way out. The spec then carries the 6 decisions below.

## Prototype changes
1. **#4 → let the user close the card during the sync.** Change: ×, Escape and a click on the scrim close the overlay while the sync runs. The sync keeps going, and the row at rest shows "Sincronizzazione in corso…" and then the result (stamp on the tile, "Ultima sincronizzazione: adesso"). Reopening during the sync shows the progress view. Check: click "Collega ora", then × right away. The card is back at rest within ~0.4 s and reads "in corso". At ~2.4 s the row reads "Ultima sincronizzazione: adesso" and the live region reads "Pagina LinkedIn collegata".
2. **#4 → a reopen cancels the success auto-close.** Change: reopening during the 950 ms success linger cancels the pending auto-close. Check: press Escape on the stamp and reopen within 0.5 s. The hub stays open until the user closes it. Today it closes itself ~400 ms after opening.

## Mechanical fixes
- **Dialog name:** `.xcard-face-expanded[role=dialog]` fails axe `aria-dialog-name` in every open state. Fix: `aria-labelledby` pointing at the head title (`ExpandCard.tsx:238`).
- **Focus escapes the `aria-modal` card:** Tab from "Collega ora" reaches "Da remoto" under the scrim, Shift+Tab from × reaches "Settore", and typing edited "Settore" while the card was open (`04-1280-focus-escaped.png`). Fix: make the page outside inert. Today only the collapsed face is (`ExpandCard.tsx:205-207`).
- **Initial focus:** Enter on the trigger opens the hub with focus on BODY. After "Collega ora", "Sovrascrivi" or "Scollega", the clicked button unmounts and focus drops to BODY. Fix: move focus to the title or the progress line.
- **Focus return:** Escape and the auto-close leave focus on BODY, and the next Tab skips the trigger (it lands on "Da remoto"). Fix: return focus to the trigger.
- **Default focus on the consequential action:** the warning and the confirm autofocus "Sovrascrivi"/"Scollega" (`VariantVetrina.tsx:144`). Pressing Enter twice from the hub row disconnected the page (trace). Fix: initial focus on "Annulla".
- **Trigger semantics (`VariantVetrina.tsx:99`):** no `aria-expanded` or `aria-haspopup="dialog"`. Its accessible name reads "Non collegatoImporta…" because the " · " separator is `aria-hidden`. Fix: add both attributes and a separator screen readers can read.
- **Trigger focus ring:** the browser's default `outline:auto 1px` is clipped to a hairline by `.xcard{overflow:hidden}` (`02-1280-trigger-focus.png`). Fix: an inset `focus-visible` ring.
- **Silent disconnect:** when the disconnect finishes, the live region goes from "Scollego la pagina LinkedIn" to "" (`machine.ts:63-78`, trace). Fix: announce "Pagina LinkedIn scollegata".
- **Small target:** × is 32×32 (`VariantVetrina.tsx:352`). Fix: a 44×44 hit area.
- **Contrast (design-system tokens, axe; all need 4.5:1 at 14 px semibold):** "Collega", "Collega ora", "Sovrascrivi": #f8fafc on #1e77eb, 4.1:1. "Gestisci": #1e77eb on #e9f1fd, 3.77:1. "Scollega": #f8fafc on #ef4444, 3.59:1.
- Violations that belong to the harness only (textarea label, `<title>`, landmarks, picker) are left out.

## Persona situations
- **Small business (not an expert), company created without LinkedIn, 390 px phone.** Will they notice the control? The filled "Collega" reads as an unfinished task even for a company with no LinkedIn page (`VariantVetrina.tsx:113-119`) → OQ1. Will they see they made progress? After the 950 ms stamp, the row shows only "linkedin.com/company/se…", so the date that proves the import is hidden (`30-iphone14-connected-rest-offline.png`) → #5.
- **Recruiting agency, `member` role, desktop:** sees an enabled "Collega" under the page's read-only notice → #6.
- **Hand-off between colleagues:** colleagues behind the same IP share the 10-requests-per-15-minutes budget with the onboarding import (`linkedin-company-import.md`, "Rate limit"). A colleague's onboarding can therefore make this card hit a 429 → #2 copy.
- **Freelance and potential freelancer:** they never reach this card. A `solo_freelancer` workspace is read-only (`access.ts:62-64`) → #6.

## Already broken around the surface
- **Stale contract table:** `company-api-contract.md` §2.3 still says PATCH silently drops `linkedin_url`. `company-edit-area-redesign/IMPLEMENTATION-NOTES.md:22` says the backend now accepts it. Do not write the spec from that table.
- **Two writers for one column:** `LinksSection.tsx:279-307` already writes `linkedin_url` from a free-text field. By the contract's own definition, a typed URL that was never scraped counts as "connected" (#1, #5).
- **No notion of outside writes:** `SettingsPage.tsx:216-236` and `useLeaveGuard.ts:34` only know about the page's own Save. A write from anywhere else is invisible to them (code only; #3).
- **No data for the last sync:** the settings page's data source leaves out `linkedin_sync_date` on purpose (`IMPLEMENTATION-NOTES.md:55`, and the frontend types do not have it). "Ultima sincronizzazione" has nothing to read (#5).
